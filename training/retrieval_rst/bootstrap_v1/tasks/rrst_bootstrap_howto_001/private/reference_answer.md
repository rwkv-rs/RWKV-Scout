Community procedure: **make the `.PortableBundle` file executable, keep it at a stable absolute path, and create `~/.local/share/applications/portablebundle-example.desktop`** [S2]. The launcher belongs under `~/.local/share/applications` and needs `Type`, `Name`, and `Exec` [S1]. The launcher requirements are official; the PortableBundle-specific procedure is community guidance and is not documented as tested on a particular Nova version [S2].
